About the Company

Who We Are

Bhumija Mines & Minerals Private Limited is a Madhya Pradesh-based mining and mineral supply company focused on responsible extraction and supply of limestone, stone aggregates, and CRM dust from the Satna–Amarpatan region.

Incorporated in October 2024 and registered with the Registrar of Companies, Gwalior, the company is led by two experienced directors bringing professional management and local expertise to the region's mineral supply chain.

The Satna district is one of MP's most active mineral belts — known for limestone, bauxite, and silica deposits — positioning Bhumija to serve growing demand from cement, infrastructure, and construction sectors.
